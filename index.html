01 / About

AI engineer with a bias for shipping.

I work where research meets real workflows — designing retrieval, reasoning and speech systems that people can actually use.

At Healtether Health, I helped build a differential-diagnosis system powered by a multi-agent framework and GraphRAG over 68,000 disease-symptom relationships extracted from SNOMED CT, AIIMS Bhubaneswar and 20+ hospitals across Tamil Nadu. I also helped cut the system's false positives by 1.2× through synthetic oversampling of rare diseases.

Later, I built a real-time doctor–patient report generator using Google's medASR model and a medical GraphRAG layer, with multilingual support for Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu. Before that, at Praxis Business School, I worked across Hadoop, Spark and classical ML pipelines.

Today I'm most at home building agentic systems — tool use, retrieval, evaluation and graph-grounded reasoning — that make complex data explainable and useful.
